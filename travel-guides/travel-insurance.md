# Travel Insurance Policy Information

## Overview
Travel insurance provides essential protection for unexpected events during your trip. Comprehensive coverage includes medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and lost baggage.

## Types of Coverage

### Medical Coverage
- **Emergency Medical Expenses**: 50,000-500,000 USD depending on policy tier
- **Evacuation Insurance**: Emergency medical evacuation (100,000-1,000,000 USD)
- **Dental**: Limited coverage for emergency dental only
- **Pharmacy**: Prescription medication coverage
- **Hospital**: Full hospitalization costs

**Important**: Pre-existing conditions typically not covered unless declared within 14 days of trip booking

### Trip Cancellation & Interruption
- **Covered Reasons**:
  - Sudden illness of traveler or family member
  - Death of family member
  - Injury preventing travel
  - Covered natural disasters
  - Job loss (within 12 months of booking)

- **Uncovered Reasons**:
  - Change of mind
  - Financial difficulty
  - Weather (standard)
  - Pre-existing conditions (unless declared)

- **Reimbursement**: Up to trip cost (advance payments, flights, hotels)
- **Typical Payout**: 80-100% of prepaid, non-refundable costs

### Baggage & Personal Belongings
- **Lost Luggage**: 1,500-3,000 USD coverage
- **Delayed Baggage**: Compensation for essentials (clothing, toiletries)
  - Usually 100-200 USD per day up to 500-1,000 USD total
- **Personal Items**: Coverage for items in luggage

**Exclusions**: Valuables without proof of ownership, fragile items, electronics (sometimes)

### Baggage Delay Insurance
- **Trigger**: Baggage delayed 12-24 hours
- **Coverage**: 100-200 USD per day for essentials
- **Limit**: Usually capped at 500-1,000 USD

### Travel Delay Insurance
- **Trigger**: Flight delay over 6-12 hours
- **Coverage**: 50-100 USD for meals and accommodation
- **Limit**: Up to 300-500 USD per claim

### Travel Misconnection
- **Coverage**: Missed connection due to covered delays
- **Reimburses**: Rerouting costs, accommodation, meals
- **Typical**: 500-1,000 USD limit

### Accidental Death & Dismemberment
- **Coverage**: 50,000-250,000 USD
- **Includes**: Accidental death during covered activities
- **Exclusions**: High-risk activities, professional sports

### Emergency Evacuation
- **Highest Coverage**: 100,000-1,000,000 USD
- **Covers**: 
  - Helicopter rescue
  - Emergency flights home
  - Medical coordination

## Coverage Limits & Costs

### Budget Plan (Basic Coverage)
- **Cost**: 50-100 USD per week
- **Includes**:
  - Basic medical (50,000 USD)
  - Trip cancellation (up to 5,000 USD)
  - Baggage loss (1,500 USD)
- **Best For**: Young, healthy travelers, short trips

### Standard Plan (Comprehensive)
- **Cost**: 150-250 USD per week
- **Includes**:
  - Medical coverage (200,000 USD)
  - Trip cancellation (up to 10,000 USD)
  - Baggage loss (2,500 USD)
  - Evacuation (500,000 USD)
- **Best For**: Most travelers, moderate risk

### Premium Plan (Maximum Protection)
- **Cost**: 300-500 USD per week
- **Includes**:
  - Medical coverage (500,000+ USD)
  - Trip cancellation (full trip cost)
  - Baggage loss (5,000 USD)
  - Evacuation (1,000,000 USD)
  - Adventure activities
- **Best For**: Expensive trips, adventure activities, older travelers

## How to Purchase

### Purchase Timing
- **Recommended**: Within 14 days of initial trip deposit
- **Latest**: Before flight departure
- **Early Purchase Benefits**: Pre-existing condition waivers, cancel for any reason options

### Where to Buy
- Insurance company websites (direct)
- Travel agent
- Credit card companies (some offer coverage)
- Tour operator packages
- Travel booking websites

### Required Information
- Trip dates
- Trip cost
- Age of travelers
- Destination country
- Pre-existing medical conditions
- Planned activities

## Making a Claim

### Documentation Required
- [ ] Original insurance policy
- [ ] Proof of payment (receipts, booking confirmations)
- [ ] Medical reports (for health claims)
- [ ] Official cancellation letters
- [ ] Airline/travel provider documentation
- [ ] Receipts for expenses (for reimbursement)

### Claim Process
1. Notify insurer within 30 days of incident
2. Submit claim form and supporting documents
3. Adjuster reviews claim (5-15 business days)
4. Approval or additional information request
5. Payment processing (10-30 days after approval)

### Average Processing Time
- Standard claims: 15-30 days
- Complex claims: 30-60 days
- Emergency claims: 24-48 hours (expedited)

## Important Exclusions

### Common Exclusions
- High-risk activities (mountaineering, skydiving)
- Travel to countries with government warnings
- Trips booked after insurance purchase
- Travel with known serious illness
- Claims while under influence
- Professional sports participation
- Claims for claimed items already recovered

### Activity-Specific Exclusions
Check policy for coverage of:
- Skiing/snowboarding
- Mountaineering
- Bungee jumping
- Professional sports
- War zones or dangerous regions

## Tips for Claims Success

1. **Document Everything**: Keep all receipts and booking confirmations
2. **Notify Early**: Report claims within recommended timeframe
3. **Understand Coverage**: Read policy details before purchase
4. **Declare Pre-existing Conditions**: Full disclosure ensures coverage
5. **Keep Records**: Original documents (not copies) for major claims
6. **Know Limits**: Understand what's covered and maximum amounts
7. **Report to Provider First**: Document incidents with airline, hotel, etc.

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Medical Emergency
- Claim Type: Medical coverage + evacuation
- Documentation: Medical reports, receipts, evacuation authorization
- Timeline: Usually covered immediately, reimbursement follows

### Scenario 2: Cancelled Trip
- Claim Type: Trip cancellation
- Documentation: Medical evidence, cancellation letter from provider
- Timeline: Can claim up to 90 days after incident

### Scenario 3: Lost Luggage
- Claim Type: Baggage loss
- Documentation: Airline baggage report (PIR), receipts, photos of items
- Timeline: After 21 days (standard baggage recovery period)

### Scenario 4: Missed Flight
- Claim Type: Travel delay
- Documentation: Airline delay certificate, receipts for expenses
- Timeline: Immediate claim after missed connection
