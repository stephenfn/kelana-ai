# Japan Visa Requirements - Travel Guide 2025

## Overview
Indonesian passport holders are required to obtain a visa to enter Japan for tourism purposes.

## Types of Visas Available

### Tourist Visa (Single/Multiple Entry)
- **Validity**: 90 days from issue date
- **Stay Duration**: Up to 30 days per entry
- **Processing Time**: 5-7 business days
- **Cost**: Approximately 3,000 JPY (~$20-25 USD)

### E-visa (Online Application)
- **Eligibility**: Indonesian citizens eligible for online application
- **Processing**: 5-7 business days online
- **Cost**: Around 4,000 JPY (~$25-30 USD)
- **Advantages**: Faster, no need to visit embassy

## Required Documents

1. **Valid Passport**
   - Must be valid for at least 6 months from date of entry
   - At least 2 blank pages required

2. **Completed Visa Application Form**
   - Form 156 (standard application)
   - Form 157 (for employment purposes)
   - Available at Japanese Embassy or online

3. **Photographs**
   - 4x6 cm passport-style photo
   - Taken within 6 months
   - White background

4. **Proof of Travel Arrangements**
   - Round-trip flight tickets or booking confirmation
   - Hotel reservations

5. **Financial Documents**
   - Bank statements (last 3-6 months)
   - Proof of sufficient funds for stay

6. **Employment Letter**
   - From current employer (if employed)
   - Including position, salary, and leave approval

## Application Process

### At Embassy
1. Submit application with documents
2. Biometric data (fingerprints)
3. Interview (if required)
4. Payment of visa fee
5. Collect passport with visa

### Online (E-visa)
1. Register on official Japan e-visa portal
2. Upload required documents
3. Complete application form
4. Make online payment
5. Receive approval and print authorization

## Important Notes

- **Visa Exemption Countries**: Japan has visa exemption agreements with some countries for stays up to 90 days - check if applicable
- **Multiple Entry**: Multiple-entry visa valid for 1 year
- **Extensions**: Visa extensions possible from within Japan (immigration office)
- **Employment**: Working in Japan requires separate work permit
