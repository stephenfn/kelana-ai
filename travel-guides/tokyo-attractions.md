# Tokyo Attractions Guide - Must-See Destinations

## Overview
Tokyo is Japan's vibrant capital and the world's most populous metropolitan area. Home to ancient temples, cutting-edge technology, and world-class dining.

## Top Attractions

### Historical & Cultural Sites

#### Senso-ji Temple (Asakusa)
- **Founded**: 645 AD (Tokyo's oldest temple)
- **Access**: Ginza Line to Tawaramachi Station
- **Hours**: Open 24/7 (grounds), buildings 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Must-See**: 
  - Massive red lantern (Chochin)
  - Nakamise shopping street
  - Five-story pagoda

#### Meiji Shrine (Shibuya)
- **Dedicated to**: Emperor Meiji and Empress Meiji
- **Access**: JR Yamanote Line to Harajuku Station
- **Hours**: Sunrise to sunset (hours vary by season)
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Best Time**: Early morning to avoid crowds
- **Features**:
  - Peaceful forest (Meiji Jingu Mori)
  - Wooden prayer tablets (ema)
  - Peaceful gardens

### Modern Attractions

#### Tokyo Skytree
- **Height**: 634 meters (world's tallest free-standing broadcast tower)
- **Location**: Sumida Ward
- **Access**: Asakusa Line to Tokyo Skytree Station
- **Hours**: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM (viewing hours vary)
- **Entry Fee**: 
  - Tembo Deck (350m): 2,100 JPY (~$15)
  - Both decks (450m + 350m): 3,100 JPY (~$22)
- **Experience**:
  - 360-degree views of Tokyo
  - Skytree Cafe
  - Shopping mall Solamachi

#### Tokyo Tower
- **Height**: 333 meters (iconic symbol of Tokyo)
- **Location**: Minato Ward
- **Built**: 1958
- **Main Deck**: 150 meters, 850 JPY (~$6)
- **Top Deck**: 250 meters, 1,600 JPY (~$11)
- **Features**:
  - Illuminated at night (red, green, or white depending on day)
  - Restaurant
  - Wax museum

### Shopping & Entertainment

#### Shibuya Crossing
- **Description**: World's busiest pedestrian crossing
- **Peak Hours**: 
  - Highest: 8:00-9:00 AM (commute)
  - Tourist friendly: 6:00-8:00 PM
- **Crossing**: Approximately 3,000 people per crossing during peak
- **Best Viewing**: Starbucks building overlooking the crossing
- **Nearby**: Shopping, restaurants, arcades

#### Akihabara Electric Town
- **Specialty**: Electronics, anime, gaming
- **Major Stores**: Yodobashi Camera, Akikake, Gachapon arcade
- **Hours**: Usually 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM (varies by store)
- **Free Entry**: Browse shops freely
- **Popular For**: Gaming culture, pop culture merchandise

### Nature & Parks

#### Ueno Park
- **Size**: 53.16 hectares
- **Entry**: Free
- **Attractions Within**:
  - Ueno Zoo (600 JPY, ~$4)
  - Tokyo National Museum (1,000 JPY, ~$7)
  - Botanical Garden (600 JPY, ~$4)
- **Best Season**: Cherry blossom season (late March - early April)
- **Facilities**: Restaurants, gift shops, rest areas

## Recommended 2-3 Day Itinerary

**Day 1 - Traditional Tokyo**
- Morning: Senso-ji Temple & Nakamise shopping
- Afternoon: Ueno Park & Museum
- Evening: Dinner in Asakusa

**Day 2 - Modern Tokyo**
- Morning: Meiji Shrine & Harajuku shopping
- Afternoon: Shibuya Crossing & shopping
- Evening: Tokyo Tower or Skytree

**Day 3 - Exploration**
- Morning: Akihabara for culture/gaming
- Afternoon: Flexible (museum, shopping, gardens)
- Evening: Last-minute shopping or dining

## Practical Information

### Transportation
- **IC Card**: Get Suica/Pasmo card at stations (2,000 JPY includes 1,500 JPY credit)
- **Trains**: Efficient and punctual (every 2-5 minutes on main lines)
- **Base Fare**: 200-300 JPY (~$1.50-2) for most trips within city

### Dining Estimates
- **Budget Ramen**: 800-1,000 JPY (~$6-7)
- **Casual Restaurant**: 2,000-3,000 JPY (~$15-22)
- **Fine Dining**: 10,000+ JPY (~$70+)

### Best Time to Visit
- **Spring** (Mar-May): Cherry blossoms, mild weather
- **Fall** (Sep-Nov): Comfortable temperature, clear skies
- **Avoid**: July-August (very hot and humid)
