# Japan Packing Checklist & Travel Tips

## Essential Documents & Valuables

### Must-Have Documents
- [ ] Passport (original + photocopy of data page)
- [ ] Visa or visa authorization
- [ ] Travel insurance document and policy number
- [ ] Flight tickets (e-tickets saved)
- [ ] Hotel reservations
- [ ] International driving permit (if renting car)
- [ ] Credit card and debit card
- [ ] Emergency contact information

### Health Documents
- [ ] Prescription medications (original bottles)
- [ ] Vaccination certificates (if required)
- [ ] Medical alert bracelet (if needed)

## Clothing by Season

### Spring (March - May)
- Lightweight jackets
- Long sleeves and pants
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Sunglasses and hat
- Umbrella (for rainy periods)

### Summer (June - August)
- Light, breathable clothing
- Shorts and t-shirts
- Swimwear
- Sun protection (SPF 50+)
- Hat and sunglasses
- Lightweight rain jacket
- Sandals

### Fall (September - November)
- Sweaters and light jackets
- Long pants and jeans
- Layering options
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Light scarf

### Winter (December - February)
- Winter coat
- Thermal underwear
- Sweaters and long sleeves
- Warm socks
- Winter boots (waterproof)
- Gloves, hat, scarf
- Umbrella

## Toiletries & Personal Care

### In Carry-On (Travel Size)
- Toothbrush and toothpaste
- Deodorant
- Razors
- Face wash
- Moisturizer
- Makeup

### In Checked Luggage
- Shampoo and conditioner (Japanese products excellent quality)
- Shower gel
- Lotion and sunscreen
- Medications
- Hair styling tools (check voltage)
- Feminine hygiene products (harder to find)

### Note
Most hotels provide basic toiletries. Department stores have excellent cosmetics.

## Electronics

### Essential
- [ ] Mobile phone and charger
- [ ] Power adapter (Japan uses Type A plugs, 100V)
- [ ] Portable power bank
- [ ] Headphones
- [ ] Camera (optional)

### Voltage Information
- Japan operates on 100V, 50Hz (Tokyo area) / 60Hz (western Japan)
- US 110V devices may work but not recommended
- Dual voltage devices: 110-240V are safe

## Luggage Packing Tips

### Recommended Luggage Size
- Carry-on: 20" x 14" x 9" (international standard)
- Checked: 25-28" for week-long trips
- Weight limit: 23kg (50lbs) per checked bag

### Packing Strategy
1. Roll clothes instead of folding to save space
2. Use packing cubes to organize by category
3. Pack shoes in shoe bags
4. Pack liquids in sealed bags (protection)
5. Keep valuables in carry-on
6. Leave extra space for purchases

### What NOT to Pack
- Large quantities of medications (customs)
- Certain foods and animal products
- Restricted items (weapons, sharp objects in carry-on)
- Expensive items that might get damaged

## Money & Payment

### Currency
- Japanese Yen (JPY)
- 1 USD ≈ 140-150 JPY (rates vary)

### Recommended
- Carry 20,000-30,000 JPY cash
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard widely accepted)
- JR Pass (if taking multiple trains)
- IC Card for local transportation (Suica, Pasmo)

### Not Needed
- Traveler's checks (difficult to exchange)
- Large amounts of foreign currency

## Before You Leave Home

- [ ] Inform your bank of travel dates
- [ ] Make copies of important documents
- [ ] Check weather forecast
- [ ] Purchase travel insurance
- [ ] Arrange airport transportation
- [ ] Set phone to airplane mode or international plan
- [ ] Adjust medications if needed
- [ ] Empty your suitcase and repack efficiently

## Souvenir Shopping Tips

### Popular Souvenirs
- Matcha tea and sweets
- Sake or Japanese whisky
- Cosmetics (Shiseido, etc.)
- Electronics
- Kimono accessories
- Regional food specialties

### Liquor Customs
- US allows 1 liter duty-free
- Additional bottles taxed
- Well-packaged for airline transport
- Declare all alcohol at customs
